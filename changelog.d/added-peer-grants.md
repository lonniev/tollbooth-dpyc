- Peer grants: a patron can let other npubs (a Parent, a Supervisor) see their account and pay for them at an Operator. The peer calls `request_peer_grant`, the patron approves the DM, and the peer calls `receive_peer_grant`. A granted peer passes `check_balance`, `account_statement`, `purchase_credits` and `check_payment` for the patron, and nothing else; paid tools still need the patron's own proof. `list_peer_grants` and `revoke_peer_grant` let the patron see and withdraw them, `held_identities` shows `peer_of`, and `forget_credentials` revokes every peer. A patron may approve several peers.
