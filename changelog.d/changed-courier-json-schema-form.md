- Secure Courier requests now carry their form as JSON Schema (draft 2020-12) in a private `form` tag on the NIP-17 message, and the operator attestation in an `attestation` tag. The text body stays as the `@@@` fallback for clients that read neither. Sensitive fields are `writeOnly` with `format: password`. The nonce and rendezvous relay are `readOnly` constants. `x-delimiter` names the text delimiter and `x-courier-kind` says whether the request is a form or an approval.
- Courier replies may be a JSON object of text values, which keeps newlines and quotes intact. `@@@` replies still work.
- `FieldSpec` gains `title` and `examples`, and `CredentialTemplate` gains `kind` (`credentials` or `approval`). The npub-proof and Authority onboarding templates are approvals, and `cache_duration` offers 2h, 1d, 1w and 30d.
- NIP-17 rumors now carry their NIP-01 `id`, so a reply can name the request it answers.
